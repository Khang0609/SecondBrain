# Ch2-Logics (HCMUT Discrete Structures Lecture)

Lecture slides from **Foundation Mathematics for Computer Science (Discrete Structures for Computing)**, Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (VNU-HCM), 41 pages.

## Coverage
1. **Propositional Logic** — Propositions, truth values, logical connectives (negation, conjunction, disjunction, implication, biconditional).
2. **Logical Equivalences** — Tautologies, contradictions, contingencies, truth-table verification, and algebraic equivalence laws.
3. **Exercises** — Practice problems on formalizing statements and proving equivalences.
