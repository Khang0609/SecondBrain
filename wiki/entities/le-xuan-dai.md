# Dr. Lê Xuân Đại

**Dr. Lê Xuân Đại** is a lecturer in the Department of Applied Mathematics, Faculty of Applied Science at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT-OISP)** (`ytkadai@hcmut.edu.vn`).

## Contributions in SecondBrain
- Author of [[inference-on-the-variances-lecture|Inference on the Variances (2025)]], covering Erlang/Gamma distributions, $\chi^2$ one-sample variance confidence intervals and tests, and $F$-distribution two-sample variance tests.
