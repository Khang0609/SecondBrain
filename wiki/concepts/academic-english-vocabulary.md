# Academic and Scientific English Vocabulary

This glossary consolidates technical, mathematical, and analytical vocabulary frequently utilized in academic literature, engineering documentation, and scientific inquiry.

## Consolidated Lexicon

### 1. Abnormal
- **Part of Speech**: Adjective
- **Vietnamese Equivalent**: *Bất thường / Dị thường*
- **Definition**: Deviating markedly from what is normal, typical, expected, or standard.
- **Academic Context**: Frequently employed in statistical data analysis to characterize outliers, anomalies, or distribution irregularities, as well as in experimental pathology.
- **Word Family**: *Abnormality* (n), *abnormally* (adv).

### 2. Adjacent
- **Part of Speech**: Adjective
- **Vietnamese Equivalent**: *Liền kề / Kề cận*
- **Definition**: Lying near, close, or contiguous; sharing a common boundary, border, vertex, or edge.
- **Academic Context**: In geometry, adjacent angles share a common vertex and arm; in graph theory, adjacent nodes are directly connected by an edge.
- **Word Family**: *Adjacency* (n), *adjacently* (adv).

### 3. Diagnose
- **Part of Speech**: Verb
- **Vietnamese Equivalent**: *Chẩn đoán*
- **Definition**: To systematically identify the nature, cause, or origin of an illness, operational malfunction, or structural failure by evaluating symptoms, diagnostic data, or test results.
- **Academic Context**: Utilized across medicine, systems engineering, root-cause analysis, and software debugging.
- **Word Family**: *Diagnosis* (n), *diagnostic* (adj), *diagnostician* (n).

### 4. Orthogonal
- **Part of Speech**: Adjective
- **Vietnamese Equivalent**: *Trực giao*
- **Definition**: Intersecting or lying at right angles ($90^\circ$); mathematically generalized to mean mutually independent, non-redundant, or uncorrelated.
- **Academic Context**: In linear algebra, two vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$ are orthogonal if their inner product equals zero ($\langle \mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v} \rangle = 0$). In software engineering, orthogonal architecture refers to subsystems that can vary independently without side effects.
- **Word Family**: *Orthogonality* (n), *orthogonally* (adv).

### 5. Perpendicular
- **Part of Speech**: Adjective
- **Vietnamese Equivalent**: *Vuông góc*
- **Definition**: Standing at right angles ($90^\circ$) to a given line, plane, or surface.
- **Academic Context**: Fundamental geometric property describing lines whose slopes satisfy $m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$ in Euclidean Cartesian coordinates. (Equipotential surfaces in [[electrical-engineering-glossary]] are always perpendicular to electric field lines).
- **Word Family**: *Perpendicularity* (n), *perpendicularly* (adv).

### 6. Plagiarized
- **Part of Speech**: Adjective / Past Participle
- **Vietnamese Equivalent**: *Đạo văn / Bị sao chép trái phép*
- **Definition**: Copied, appropriated, or closely imitated from the language, ideas, or artistic expressions of another author without explicit attribution or legitimate acknowledgment.
- **Academic Context**: Represents a grave violation of academic integrity and scientific ethics.
- **Word Family**: *Plagiarism* (n), *plagiarize* (v), *plagiarist* (n).

## Technical Distinction: Orthogonal vs. Perpendicular

While colloquially treated as synonyms, scientific discourse maintains a distinct conceptual difference:
- **Perpendicular**: Specifically geometric; describes physical lines, rays, or planes intersecting at a $90^\circ$ Euclidean angle.
- **Orthogonal**: A broader mathematical and abstract generalization. It encompasses geometric perpendicularity while extending to abstract vector spaces (orthogonal functions, orthogonal polynomials, Fourier series), statistical independence (orthogonal factors in regression), and software decoupling.
