# Chemical Equilibrium

**Chemical equilibrium** occurs in reversible reactions when the forward and reverse reaction rates become equal, resulting in constant macroscopic concentrations.

## 1. Reversible Reactions
Reversible reactions do not go to completion and proceed simultaneously in both directions:
$$a\text{A} + b\text{B} \rightleftharpoons c\text{C} + d\text{D}$$

## 2. Equilibrium Constants ($K_c$ and $K_p$)
- **Concentration Equilibrium Constant ($K_c$):**
  $$K_{\text{eq}} = K_c = \frac{k_f}{k_r} = \frac{[\text{C}]^c [\text{D}]^d}{[\text{A}]^a [\text{B}]^b}$$
- **Pressure Equilibrium Constant ($K_p$):** Expressed in terms of partial pressures of gaseous species only (pure solids and liquids have constant density/activity and are omitted):
  $$K_p = \frac{P_{\text{C}}^c P_{\text{D}}^d}{P_{\text{A}}^a P_{\text{B}}^b}$$
- **Relationship between $K_p$ and $K_c$:**
  $$K_p = K_c (RT)^{\Delta n}$$
  where $\Delta n = (\text{moles of gaseous products}) - (\text{moles of gaseous reactants})$.
